`gate: passed 2026-09-02`. What that stamp rests on, recorded so it can be audited rather than
taken on trust.

## Adversary layer (PLAN-ADVERSARY, via /codex-researcher)

Ten rounds. Findings by round: 8 · 6 · 5 · 5 · 1 · 0 · 0 · 0 · 3.

Clean passes at rounds 6, 7 and 9 — three consecutive, spanning the restatement pass and every
factual correction made after it. The inflection is at round 5→6, where implementation *mechanism*
was withdrawn from the PRD and restated as constraints, on the finding that mechanism can only be
settled with code in hand.

Round 10 returned three findings, all **under-specification** introduced by a compliance pass that
over-applied a brief-scoped authoring rule to a PRD: the per-site classification the PRD promised
did not exist on disk, acceptance had no runnable procedure, and the frontend timeout decision was
satisfiable by a no-op. All three were repaired before this stamp — the classification was
reconstructed keyed by owning function, acceptance became `just test-under-load`, and the frontend
contract became behavioural.

## Completeness layer (SPEC-GATE, via cold-reader)

Ten rounds. The design dimensions have passed since round 5. Findings after that point were, with
one exception, incorrect factual claims by the author — seven occasions, including two corrections
that were themselves wrong. Each was verified against the repository and fixed.

The exception is round 10's data/schema finding ("several tests" where one test asserts), also
fixed.

## What the stamp does not claim

The final round of repairs — the classification table, the acceptance recipe, the frontend
contract, and the stripping of file paths from `## Implementation Decisions` — was **not** re-gated
by either layer. The maintainer made an explicit decision to stop gating at that point, on the
grounds that ten rounds had produced no change to the plan since round 5 and that further rounds
were oscillating rather than converging.

The residual risk is that supporting prose in this PRD contains an error neither layer saw. That
risk is bounded by the fact that the epic's completion is decided by a command
(`just test-under-load`) rather than by this document.
